# Right-hand and Left-hand Rules

| Rule | Use it for | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Right-hand (grip) | direction of $B$ around a wire | thumb = current, fingers = field |
| Left-hand | direction of force $F_m$ | field enters the palm, fingers = current, thumb = force |

1. find the direction of the current (or the velocity of a positive charge)
2. find the direction of the field
3. apply the rule
