# Charged Particle in a Magnetic Field

If $\vec v \perp \vec B$, the magnetic force acts as a centripetal force:
$$Q v B = \frac{m v^2}{r} \;\Rightarrow\; r = \frac{m v}{Q B}$$

- the particle moves on a **circle**
- the magnetic force does no work, so the speed stays constant
- used in mass spectrometers and cyclotrons

> [!question] Think about it
> What path does the particle take if $\vec v$ is not perpendicular to $\vec B$?
