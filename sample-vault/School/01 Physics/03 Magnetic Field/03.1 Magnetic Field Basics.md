# Magnetic Field Basics

- created by moving charges (currents) and by permanent magnets
- field lines are closed loops, they leave the north pole and enter the south pole
- symbol of the induction: $B$, unit: tesla $\mathrm{T}$

> [!tip] Earth
> The Earth itself is a huge magnet. The geographic north pole is close to the magnetic **south** pole.

## Material types
- **diamagnetic** – weakly repelled
- **paramagnetic** – weakly attracted
- **ferromagnetic** – strongly attracted, can be magnetized (iron, nickel, cobalt)
