# Electric Field

> A type of force field that surrounds ==every== electrically charged object.
> Overview of the topic. Details are in the sub-notes below.

## Main formulas
| Quantity | Formula | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Electric charge | $Q = n\cdot e$ | C |
| Coulomb force | $F_e=\dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r}\cdot\dfrac{\vert Q_1 Q_2\vert}{r^2}$ | N |
| Field strength | $E=\dfrac{F_e}{q}$ | N/C |
| Capacitance | $C=\dfrac{Q}{U}$ | F |
