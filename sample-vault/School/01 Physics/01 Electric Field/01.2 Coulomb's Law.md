# Coulomb's Law

> The force between two point charges is proportional to the product of the charges and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them.

- electric force
- symbol: $F_e$
- unit: $\mathrm{N}$
- formula:
$$F_e = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r}\cdot\frac{|Q_1 Q_2|}{r^2}$$

> $\varepsilon_0 = 8.85\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm{F\,m^{-1}}$ (permittivity of vacuum)
> $\varepsilon_r \ge 1$ (relative permittivity)
> - vacuum: $\varepsilon_r = 1$
> - air: $\varepsilon_r \approx 1$
> - water: $\varepsilon_r \approx 80$

![[two-charges.svg|420]]

> [!example] Example
> **Given:** $Q_1 = 2\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, $Q_2 = -3\,\mathrm{\mu C}$, $r = 0.3\,\mathrm{m}$, in air.
> **Find:** $F_e = ?$
>
> $$F_e = 8.99\times10^{9}\cdot\frac{2\times10^{-6}\cdot3\times10^{-6}}{0.3^2} \approx 0.60\,\mathrm{N}$$
>
> **Result:** $\boxed{F_e \approx 0.6\,\mathrm{N}}$, attractive.
