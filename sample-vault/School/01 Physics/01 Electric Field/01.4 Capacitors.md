# Capacitors

A capacitor stores charge (and energy) between two conducting plates.

$$C = \frac{Q}{U} \qquad C = \varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r\frac{S}{d}$$

| Connection | Total capacitance |
| --- | --- |
| parallel | $C = C_1 + C_2 + \dots$ |
| series | $\dfrac{1}{C} = \dfrac{1}{C_1} + \dfrac{1}{C_2} + \dots$ |

Energy stored: $W = \tfrac12 C U^2$

> [!warning] Watch out
> Series and parallel are the **opposite** of resistors.
