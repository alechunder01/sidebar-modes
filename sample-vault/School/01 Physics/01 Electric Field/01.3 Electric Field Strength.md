# Electric Field Strength

- symbol: $E$
- unit: $\mathrm{N/C} = \mathrm{V/m}$
- vector quantity, points in the direction of the force on a **positive** test charge
$$E = \frac{F_e}{q}$$

For a point charge:
$$E = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\cdot\frac{|Q|}{r^2}$$

## Field lines
- start on positive charges, end on negative ones
- never cross
- denser lines mean a stronger field
