# Electric Charge

- symbol: $Q$
- unit: coulomb, $\mathrm{C}$
- charge is **quantized**: $Q = n\cdot e$, with $e = 1.602\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}$
- charge is **conserved** in an isolated system

> [!note] Two kinds of charge
> Like charges repel, unlike charges attract.

## Ways to charge an object
1. friction (rubbing a rod with cloth)
2. contact (touching a charged conductor)
3. induction (no contact needed)

## Summary
- the electron carries the smallest free charge
- insulators keep charge in place, conductors let it move
