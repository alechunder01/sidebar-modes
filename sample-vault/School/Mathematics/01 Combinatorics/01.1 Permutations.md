# Permutations

## Definition
> An arrangement of all $n$ elements in a row.

$$P(n) = n!$$

With repetition (groups of equal elements $n_1, n_2, \dots$):
$$P'(n_1,\dots,n_k) = \frac{n!}{n_1!\cdot\dots\cdot n_k!}$$

> [!example] Example
> In how many ways can the letters of the word `NETWORK` be arranged?
> All 7 letters differ, so $P(7) = 7! = 5040$.
