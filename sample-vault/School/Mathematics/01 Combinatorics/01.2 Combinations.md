# Combinations

Choose $k$ elements out of $n$, **order does not matter**:
$$C(n,k) = \binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{k!\,(n-k)!}$$

| Order matters? | Repetition? | Name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| yes | no | variation $V(k,n)$ |
| no | no | combination $C(k,n)$ |

> [!warning] Watch out
> Decide first whether order matters. That one question settles most exercises.
