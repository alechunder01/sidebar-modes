# Entrepreneurship

- **entrepreneur** – a person who runs a business on their own risk, to make a profit
- profit = revenue − costs

| Form | Liability | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sole trader | unlimited | simplest to start |
| Limited company | limited to the contribution | needs capital and more paperwork |

## Business plan checklist
- [x] idea and target customers
- [x] competitors
- [ ] price and costs
- [ ] funding
