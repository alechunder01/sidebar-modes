# Economics
- the science of how people handle scarce resources

## Basic terms
### Needs
- a feeling of lack that we try to remove
- **material**: food, clothes, a phone
- **immaterial**: friendship, education, safety

### Goods and services
- **good** – a thing (free: air; scarce: pizza, a car)
- **service** – human activity that satisfies someone else's need, it can't be stored (hairdresser, taxi driver)

### Production factors
1. labour
2. land
3. capital

> [!note] Opportunity cost
> The value of the best alternative you give up when you choose something.
